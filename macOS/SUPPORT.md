# ADI-2 Companion support

ADI-2 Companion is independent Mac software for EQ profiles and ADI-2 MIDI
control. It is not RME's official Remote application and is not affiliated with
or endorsed by RME.

## Contact the maintainer

For app questions, problems, feedback or feature requests, contact **Spencer
Norton**, the project maintainer, through the
[ADI-2 Companion issue tracker](https://github.com/spencernorton/rme-adi2-linux/issues/new).
Sign in to GitHub, open an issue and describe what you need help with. The
maintainer can reply in that issue. This is a public support channel; a GitHub
account is used by the website, not by the app.

Do not post passwords, purchase receipts, payment details, device serial numbers,
private EQ files, or other confidential information. A useful bug report includes
app version, macOS version, Mac architecture, ADI-2 model/firmware and clear steps
to reproduce the problem. Describe the result you expected and what happened.

## Setup and app information

- [Setup, requirements and features](README.md)
- [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)

Hardware control needs a USB-connected ADI-2 with compatible firmware and MIDI
Control enabled on the device. Offline EQ editing and the clearly marked demo
mode are available without hardware. Demo mode does not control a physical DAC.

The first paid Mac release is being prepared around ADI-2 DAC live EQ, volume and parameter
controls, offline editing, and local profiles. Choose **Local profiles** to save
or load an EQ draft on your Mac; loading a profile does not change hardware
until you choose Apply. Pro/2/4 hardware has not been qualified with this app.

The app does not operate a user account system, collect telemetry or upload EQ
profiles. Saved local profiles remain on your Mac unless you export/share them.
See the privacy policy for the complete explanation and local-data removal.
